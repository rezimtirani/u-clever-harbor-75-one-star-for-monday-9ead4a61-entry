A one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant: an hour's wait for a table, a waiter who serves a full week, and a bill that takes the whole weekend.

> One star. Waited an hour for a table, the waiter served me a full week, and the bill took my entire weekend.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 180 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line, at most 25 words.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
